Program structure

Training runs in 4-week blocks, counted strictly week-wise — not by calendar month or fixed start/end dates.

Class days

Classes are held Monday to Friday only. No classes on Saturday or Sunday.

Payment schedule

Payment for the next 4-week block is due at the start of Week 5 of the current block.

Refund policy

No refunds are issued under any circumstances.

If a class is cancelled by the trainer

A makeup class will be scheduled to cover it. Payment still continues on the normal week-wise schedule regardless of makeup classes.

If a class is cancelled by the client

  • You must inform the trainer at least 1 hour before the scheduled class.
  • No notice, or less than 1 hour's notice — that class is counted as done, no makeup.
  • Notice given in time — once payment for the next block (Week 5) is made, you may take a makeup class on your own, whenever you're free.
